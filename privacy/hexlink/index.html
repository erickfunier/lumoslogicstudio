HexLink privacy policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Lumos Logic Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) built HexLink as a free-to-play game. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

Information stored on your device

HexLink stores game progress, settings, star ratings, and move counts locally on your device so you can continue playing. This information is part of the game experience and is not intentionally sent to us.

Optional Google Play Games services

If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games may provide achievements, leaderboards, and Saved Games synchronization. These services are optional and are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

HexLink may show banner advertisements through Unity LevelPlay. Advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and approximate location to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to their policies and applicable consent requirements.

Analytics

Unity Analytics is disabled in the current HexLink configuration. We do not intentionally collect analytics events through Unity Analytics.

Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, audio, camera data, or precise location through HexLink.

Children’s privacy

HexLink is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to lumoslogicstudio@gmail.com.
