HexLink privacy policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Lumos Logic Studio built HexLink as a free-to-play game. This page explains what information the game handles and why.

Information stored on your device

HexLink stores game progress, settings, star ratings, and move counts locally on your device. This information is not intentionally sent to us.

Optional Google Play Games services

If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games may provide achievements, leaderboards, and Saved Games synchronization. These services are optional and governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

HexLink may show banner advertisements through Unity LevelPlay. Advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, and approximate location subject to their policies and applicable consent requirements.

Analytics and information we do not intentionally collect

Unity Analytics is disabled in the current configuration. We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, contacts, photos, audio, camera data, or precise location through HexLink.

Contact

Questions can be sent to lumoslogicstudio@gmail.com.
